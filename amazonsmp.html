    Rules (2026/06/26)

    Rules (and punishment in brackets)

    No Cheating / Hacking (ban 2 to 8 days).

    No Administrator Power Abuse (ban 2 to 8 days, and administrator permissions revoked).

    No Inappropriate Content* (ban 1 to 4 days).

    No Griefing* (ban 1 to 4 days).

    Conditions

    Strong probable cause and suspicion is enough to consider someone guilty (however evidence is preferred). Breaking a rule will result in a ban, Admins will discuss and vote on ban length (if an Admin is being banned, said Admin will not be able to participate in the discussion and vote).

    Bans for rules with a star (*) will only occur if the victim reports it and requests for the offender to be banned. However, these cases will each be considered individually by admins, any retaliation and actions leading up to and after the offence should be sent to an Admin. Also, Admins will attempt to solve the issue without banning anyone, but if it can not be resolved simply then a ban will occur.

    'No Inappropriate Content' can refer to a lot of things, but mainly: discrimination (https://www.gov.uk/discrimination-your-rights), sexual content, sensitive topics (like politics, war and religion), content generated by artificial intelligence, doxing, impersonation, and severe disrespect.

    Final ban time formula: [final ban time] = [past bans count] * [current offense ban time].

    New Members (2026/06/26)

    Criteria

    Must own an official copy of 'Minecraft: Java Edition' or 'Minecraft: Bedrock Edition', and have the ability to play online.

    Must know at least two current Members personally in real life (These two Members will be called this person's 'Inviting Members'), (each of those two chosen Inviting Members must know at least one Admin personally in real life). Or, must know at least one current Admin personally in real life, (this Admin will be called this person's 'Inviting Members', (plural spelling is used for simplicity).).

    Must not have had a Permanent Ban in the past.

    Joining

    The Members inviting (called Inviting Members) the Applicant (person attempting to become a Member) should inform an Admin of the Applicant's wish to join.

    If all criteria is met, and they are approved by Dohst (https://github.com/dohst0) and/or by the community: then the Applicant should read the 'Rules and Conditions', 'Privacy Notice' and 'Community Information' sections/documents on the AmazonSMP Page on the Dohst Website (https://dohst0.github.io/amazonsmp.html#information), and then complete the Registration Form (https://forms.gle/viLDVgURCKZXF6wn9).

    After Dohst (https://github.com/dohst0) confirms the Applicant's registration and approval, the Applicant will be considered a New Member and may be whitelisted by an Admin (if the Member is on Bedrock Edition, then an Admin may have to turn off the whitelist for the first time they join).

    Conditions

    Please note that completing this registration form for someone else, and/or entering false information is not allowed. If this is found to have happened then the submission will not be counted.

    For the 30 days after a New Member joins, the Inviting Members are responsible for the New Member's Actions. Meaning that if the New Member is banned for anything, then the Inviting Members will also be banned for the same time period and reason. (Inviting Members refers to the two current Members who chose to invite said New Member).

    Data Storage (2026/08/21)

    Storage

    All of your data will be stored with AES-256 encryption and LZMA2 compression. Except for you 'Name' (specifically only 'minecraft username and uuid'), 'Server Log and Player Data' and 'Internet Protocol Address' which is also stored in some minecraft server files.

    These will be stored only on the server host machine.

    Collection

    Data is only collected starting from after you submit the 'Member Registration' form, or from after you join the server for the first time.

    All data which was collected in the past but is no longer listed in the above table will be deleted.

    Request

    You can request to see what data has been collected about you. To do this follow the same instructions as for 'Data Deletion From Request', but specify that you want to see your data instead of deleting it.

    Data Deletion (2026/08/21)

    From Request

    You can request for your data to be deleted at any time. To do this please contact Dohst or create a new issue on the github project.

    You will have to provide some evidence to prove your identity as the person who you wish to delete data for, (this does NOT mean personal identification which is used in real life, please do NOT dox yourself), (the easiest way is to directly contact Dohst (https://github.com/dohst0) with a direct message on Discord (application) or Whatsapp (application)).

    Please specify what data you want to be deleted, and from what time range. Also note that if you want absolutely all of your data deleted then every single trace of your existence on the sever (including all backups) will be deleted.

    Please remember that this data is collected for the maintenance of the server. If you ask for your data to be deleted you will no longer be a Member (and so you will not be able to join the server unless you rejoin as a New Member), unless you only request for 'optional' data to be deleted, or if you only only request for your data to be deleted from backups but not the present save.

    After submitting your request, your data will be deleted within 7 days. If you requested for your data to be removed from backups, it will take up to 14 days for it to all be deleted.

    From Leaving / Being Removed (this is not the same as being banned)

    When leaving or being removed from the sever, some data will be deleted, however some will be kept incase you decide to join back. If you want more of your data deleted at any time, you will have to submit a request, as for 'Data Deletion From Request'.

    Data which is deleted: 'Inviters', 'Join Date', 'Birth Year', 'Internet Protocol Address', 'Devices', 'Minecraft Edition' and 'Name' (specifically only 'online name', 'last name' and 'Discord username').

    Data which is kept: 'Name' (specifically only 'first/preferred real name' and 'minecraft username and uuid'), 'Server Log and Player Data'.

    From Being Permanently Banned

    Absolutely all of your data and every single trace of your existence on the sever (including all backups) will be deleted.

    This will be done within 14 days of the person being banned.
